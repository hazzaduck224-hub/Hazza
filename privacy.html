No Exit Protocol · Discord Bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026
In short: the bot only uses the Discord information it needs to run the server. It doesn't sell your data, doesn't keep your messages, and stores almost nothing.

01Information the bot uses

While running, the bot can see standard Discord information about members of the server, such as:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar.
  • Your roles in the server, and when you joined.
  • When your Discord account was created, to block very new accounts during raids.
  • Messages you send in the server, which are checked as they arrive to detect spam.
  • Reactions you add to the role panel.

02What is stored

  • Server settings only: message and channel IDs for the bot's panels, the game status text, and similar configuration.
  • Messages are not saved. For spam detection, recent messages are kept in memory for about 30 seconds and then discarded. They are cleared whenever the bot restarts.
  • Security logs: when the bot takes a moderation action (for example removing spam), it posts a log in a private staff channel. The log includes the member's username, user ID, account age and the reason.
  • Welcome images are created from your username and avatar and posted in the welcome channel. They aren't stored separately.

03How it's used

Information is used only to run the bot's features: giving roles, welcoming members, showing server statistics, and keeping the server safe from raids and spam. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't shared with anyone outside the server's staff except as described below.

04Third parties

  • Discord provides the platform the bot runs on. See the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • Bloxlink handles Roblox account verification as a separate service with its own privacy policy. This bot only sees the role Bloxlink gives you, not your Roblox login.

05Keeping and deleting data

Most information isn't stored by the bot at all. Security logs stay in the staff channel until staff remove them. If you leave the server, the bot stops seeing your information. To ask for logs about you to be removed, contact the server owner.

06Children

Discord requires users to meet a minimum age (13 in most countries). The bot is only for people who meet Discord's age requirements.

07Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time, and the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions? Contact the server owner or a member of staff in the No Exit Protocol Discord server.
